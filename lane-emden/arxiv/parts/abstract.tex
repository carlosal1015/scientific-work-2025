Lane-Emden-type equations are a class of nonlinear singular
second-order ordinary differential equations arising from the
modeling of various physical phenomena, including the internal
structure of self-gravitating polytrophic bodies, thermal explosions,
and chemical reactions.
This paper leverages the Adomian Decomposition Method, a robust
algorithm for handling singularities at the origin as a
semi-analytical approach.
In other hand, we employ the integration methods and physics-informed
machine learning to compare with these solutions.
We validate our approach through numerical experiments whose results
align with the error analysis presented herein.
