Lane-Emden-type equations are a class of singular second-order
nonlinear ordinary differential equations (ODEs) arising from the
modeling of various physical phenomena, including the internal
structure of self-gravitating polytropic bodies, thermal explosions,
and chemical reactions.
This paper leverages the Adomian Decomposition Method, a robust algorithm for handling singularities at the origin.
Additionally, we employ deep learning to approximate solutions of Lane-Emden-type equations using physics-informed machine learning.
We validate our approach through numerical experiments whose results align with the theoretical analysis presented herein.
